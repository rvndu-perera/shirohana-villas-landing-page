About Us

Eight Curated Eco Luxury Escapes

Shirohana Villas offers a collection of eight private eco-luxury escapes across Sri Lanka. Our portfolio spans the misty highlands of Haputale and Nuwara Eliya; the coastal soul of Kalpitiya, Kosgoda, Negombo, and Bentota; the historic landscape of Kirinda; and the quiet wilderness of Kaleliya.

Each property features distinct architecture, style, and curated experiences, defined by its sense of place, while delivering our signature comfort and serenity.
